# Finish gaps on a saved KiCad project

Open a complete saved project in Copperpath and wait for its board to appear. Then choose **Route saved board**, keep **Finish gaps**, and preview routing. The board stays beside the verified targets and protected count. The Findings tab carries KiCad’s full results. Changing the source or settings requires a new preview. Review the copper changes and findings, then accept and export a freshly checked separate project. [Routing settings](routing-preferences.md)

The earlier Finish gaps jobs fixed their existing routes but left every net active in Freerouting. The corrected workflow uses KiCad’s missing connections after refill and full-schematic checks. Copperpath maps both endpoints to the same saved net and puts the other nets in ignored engine classes. Their pins, rules and obstacles stay in the engine input, including applicable planes. Extra optimization is disabled. Before delivery, Copperpath verifies that every original route is exact and that new copper belongs to the reviewed nets. The admitted scope uses two supported outer copper layers, a matching saved schematic and exact endpoint and terminal identities.
