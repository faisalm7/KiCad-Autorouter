# Runtime bounds and retained research

Routing runs inspect the active job directory, not the accumulated research archive. `pipeline.check_storage(active_root=output)` performs a fresh bounded descriptor-based scan of that exact directory and checks at least2GiB free on both the checkout and output filesystems. The active job retains the18GiB logical-size ceiling,15second scan deadline and500000entry bound. Directory symlinks are refused/not traversed; missing fresh output is allowed before creation, while errors after a scope exists reject. Nested trial growth is measured again on every call. Per-worker time, log, RSS and disk bounds are unchanged and typically much smaller. Source snapshots, retained candidate proof and original-design checks are not omitted.

The no-argument `check_storage()` remains an explicit aggregate `.data`/`.tools`/`.runs` archive audit for setup/research administration. Runtime callers use the active-root form. The18GiB aggregate is therefore **not** a current claim about all historical files during each route. Retained evidence is preserved separately and is not needed to route a new board. Free-space guards remain sampled and can race other writers; they are not filesystem quotas. Process groups do not contain deliberately detached hostile descendants. No guarantee covers a hostile same-user process, supervisor SIGKILL or power loss.

These are different scopes: they demonstrate the removed historical dependency, **not** an end-to-end routing speedup. Raw process receipts/logs are retained in `.runs/storage-profile-before-001` and `.runs/storage-profile-active-001`. A source/installed/native qualification milestone must validate the changed runtime before a new installation is called qualified.
