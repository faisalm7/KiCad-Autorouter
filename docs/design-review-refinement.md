# Design review and refinement

## Reference and design decision

The saved-project workspace will keep the board on the left and the current task on the right. A layer selector, fit and zoom controls, and a keyboard-accessible item selector will sit with the board. Selecting an item will explain its reference, pad, net or copper role and highlight its location. Findings with verified item identities can locate geometry. The review will show the next decision and keep the request and history accessible. Closed-project checks, approvals and delivery continue through the shared engineering core.

It reported delivery at 349.135 seconds and independent readback at 366.397 seconds. Those times include observer interruptions. Seven inherited warnings remained visible and no CAD corrections were made. At 880 × 610 its copper display used small pad centres and short labels, and the conversation opened on a dense settings summary. The follow-up `a7542e33f` installation checked opening a fresh separate export in KiCad. [Measured reference](../evidence/product-guided-board-journey-002.json) · [Guided journey](guided-board-journey.md)

Extend the current board-coordinate drawing, wx controls and raster backend. Drawing and picking can use one coordinate transform. Bounded, supported pad and outline geometry can be drawn from the saved PCB and matched by identity. Mark centre-only shapes and schematic symbols for display where needed. The existing KiCad plots include real copper and zone fill but carry no board-item IDs. On the two retained examples their plotted drill centres align after shifting by the outline origin, while the plotted viewport dimensions differ from the board extents. Keep these plots available through the detailed report. KiCanvas is an interesting embeddable viewer, but the documented embedding path does not establish the identity-based finding, approval or editor synchronization this journey needs. Keep the native wx app and avoid another viewer dependency for this milestone. [KiCad command-line reference](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/cli/cli.pdf) · [wx SVG rendering](https://docs.wxpython.org/wx.svg.html) · [KiCanvas embedding](https://kicanvas.org/embedding/)

The saved viewer shows a saved or separately checked state. The existing synchronized companion can read live editor selection and apply the qualified KiCad 10.0.8 move with native Undo. That operation remains separate. KiCad 10 connects add-ons to the running editor through IPC. Its public interface has no documented docking API and cannot plot the running editor through IPC. Maintain the existing exact handoff. A true embedded panel, live route overlays, general live edits and schematic edits require their own implementation and qualification. Open the saved PushButtonLed project. Enter the recorded placement request, review the proposed area and inspect the affected copper before approving the moves. Review the routing plan and start routing. The board in **Copper changes** shows the checked candidate. Choose the copper layer, zoom in, and type a part reference or exact net in the item field. An exact net search marks the items shown on the current layer. Blue lines follow straight copper present in the displayed board. Removed traces keep their dashes and get location markers. Inspect any marked item for its copper role and use KiCad for full layer geometry. Use **Refine request** to return to the saved project with the earlier request and the inspected net, item or finding in view. Change the request and preview again before approving anything. A changed or locked project requires a fresh inspection. A checked delivery opens as a separate KiCad project. Use **Back to workspace** to retain the current placement draft without refining it.
