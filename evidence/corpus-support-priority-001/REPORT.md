The ideal-diode source has two ordinary UUID zones, 101 tracks and 12 vias. Its project has no unknown severities, and the current static guard rejects only its original format. It has a `gr_rect` outline. Do not route it through the placement policy merely by adding a fabricated area; ordinary native-input routing is the appropriate first admission path. Its four repeated transistor channels count as one board, not four independent families.

PAW's original diagnostic001 remains failed on disposable PRL preservation. The separate protected-input diagnostic001 actually completed its canary, positive/invalid controls and unchanged PAW DRC, retaining zero opens, zero blocking findings, 17 warnings and no exclusions. That result does not establish routing or legacy error enforcement. Existing zone controls demonstrated that KiCad omits the old error even for assigned padless copper; use the measured independent membership predicate instead of dropping the key. Actual PAW `GetPadCount` and final-candidate enforcement have not run.

LiPo's completed002 observation already binds its ten pads, three nets and eight routes and reports zero opens, zero blocking findings and seven warnings. Do not repeat it. The existing format plan records the precise next missing save/refill question and its UUID-only helper failure. Two original zones explicitly contain fixed legacy teardrop intent; the third uses `F&B.Cu`. A global version or ID rewrite would silently broaden unsupported strategies. Floor, placement and pruning should remain blocked unless their separate old-format contracts are proved.
