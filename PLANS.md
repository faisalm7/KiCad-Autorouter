Keep the supported separate-project path and the experimental owned-board preview. [Editor capability and source audit](docs/live-editor-milestone.md).

Keep live docking/schematic operations, Windows Native, independent installation, human/electrical validation, and full/off-Mac recovery separate from the supported saved-project workflow.
