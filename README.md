# KiCad-Autorouter — local reconstruction

This checkout starts a new, locally backed research and engineering effort. It currently contains instructions and historical reference material, not an implemented router or verified benchmark improvement.

Read `START_HERE.md` to prepare the local session. `MASTER_PROMPT.md` is the complete engineering mandate, and `AGENTS.md` contains persistent guardrails. Historical files in `.local-reference/` are not current results and must not be published automatically.

The intended research goal is higher native-validated routing completion on ordinary KiCad boards under fair budgets, with preserved constraints and usable routes. No success rate, novelty, compatibility, or release-readiness claim has yet been established in this reconstruction.
